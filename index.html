🍔 Overcooked Lite

Serve as many orders as you can. The orders get harder. The kitchen doesn't get bigger.

Play online

Host shares the 4-letter code (or a link). 2–4 players, one per browser.

Same screen

Level

Players 1–3 share the keyboard; any player can use a gamepad.

How to play
  • Grab ingredients from the crates along the top wall. Space / Q picks up or puts down whatever you're facing.
  • Chop lettuce & tomato on a chopping board — hold E.
  • Cook patties on a stove (left wall). Don't let them burn!
  • Plate it: grab a plate from the rack, then pick up the ingredients onto it. Match the ticket and hit SERVE.
  • Wash dirty plates at the sink (hold E) — you only have a few plates.
  • Shift to dash. Fast serves earn tips; streaks multiply points.
  • Orders pile up faster and recipes get bigger as the clock runs.
